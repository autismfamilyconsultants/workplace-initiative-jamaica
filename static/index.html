The initiative

From school to meaningful work.

Autism Family Consultants is developing a workforce initiative for autistic and otherwise neurodivergent young adults in Jamaica.

Through a micro-franchise secured with EcoWash Global, PodWash Jamaica will create an eco-friendly setting for workforce training, skill building, and sustainable employment.
